# Code of conduct

Kernox contributors must keep technical collaboration respectful, specific,
and free from harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of private
information. Debate ideas and evidence; do not attack people.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed in
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take
other proportionate action to protect the community. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest recused.
